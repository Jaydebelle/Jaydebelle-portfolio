03 — PSYCHOLOGY

Why psychology?

One question continues to stay with me:

Why do people think, feel and behave the way they do?

Psychology interests me because human behaviour is complex. The same situation can affect different people in completely different ways, and there is often much more beneath an individual's behaviour than what we can immediately observe.

I want to continue developing my understanding of psychological concepts through formal education, independent learning, research and real-world experiences.
